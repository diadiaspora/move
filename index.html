✳   THE DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Still wondering about something? Add it to your quote request and our team can help.

How far in advance should I book?+

Booking 2–4 weeks ahead is a useful starting point, especially for weekends and month-end dates. Availability varies, so contact us as soon as you know your date.

Do you provide packing materials?+

Packing materials can be included in a full-service package or added to your move. Confirm materials and costs in your written estimate.

Can you move large or fragile items?+

Tell us about pianos, antiques, artwork, safes, or specialty items before booking. Some need special equipment or specialist handling.

Are the prices on this page final?+

No. These are sample starting prices. Your actual quote depends on inventory, distance, access, crew size, timing, and services.

Do you offer insurance or valuation coverage?+

Coverage options and liability rules depend on the mover and location. Ask for available options, limitations, and written terms before your move.
